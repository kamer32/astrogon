# System Override: Cyberpunk Overhaul & Performance Patch

Pilots, the Taurus Sector has undergone a massive architectural and technical transformation. Driven by telemetry data and upcoming festival preparations, we have fully embraced a dark cyber-dystopian visual identity while heavily optimizing core systems and boss encounters.

This update is not just a collection of bug fixes; it is a complete restructuring of the visual and technical matrix of **Beat Taurus**. Here is the current compilation status for the newly deployed modules:

### [█████████░] 90% - Cybernetic World & Environmental Assets

The Taurus Sector now features a fully realized, neon-drenched, and foreboding aesthetic.

* **Cyberpunk Theme Integration:** All sectors have been upgraded with glowing neon billboards, propaganda displays for the Apostles of Ash, and eerie biomechanical architectural elements.
* **New Environmental Assets:** Added interconnected bio-cables, power sockets, floating inverted-cone monoliths with emissive energy cracks, and vertical city structures to maximize depth.
* **High-Res Texture Overhaul:** All environmental and structural textures have been upgraded to high-resolution materials, eliminating pixelation and ensuring crisp visual fidelity.

### [████████░░] 80% - Boss Encounters & Performance Tuning

High-intensity boss fights and rendering loops have been streamlined for maximum frame stability.

* **Boss Fight Optimization:** CPU/GPU spikes during multi-phase boss transitions and dense bullet-hell salvos have been neutralized. Encounters now maintain a rock-solid 60 FPS.
* **Hit Response & Visuals:** Sustained fire on bosses now triggers enhanced emissive crack flashes and stage-phase destruction sequences.
* **Bug Fixes:** Resolved bullet collision glitches, animation locks, and out-of-bounds exploits across all main arenas.

### [█████████░] 90% - Material & Render Efficiency (Draw Call Reduction)

Engine-level tweaks have been deployed to minimize CPU/GPU overhead.

* **Material Consolidation:** Optimized material assignment across hundreds of environmental props. Opague hulls and glowing emissive lines now share unified palettes, drastically reducing Draw Calls.
* **Static Batching:** All static environmental structures, cables, and platforms have been batched, neutralizing unnecessary CPU transform calculations.
